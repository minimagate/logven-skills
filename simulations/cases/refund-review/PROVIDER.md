The payment gateway accepts `charge_id` and an integer count of the currency's smallest unit. Supported currencies include USD (2 decimals), JPY (0 decimals), and KWD (3 decimals). IOError means a connection could not be established and no refund was accepted. A 202 response tells clients the refund has been accepted for processing. Invoice IDs are globally unique; account IDs supplied to the endpoint come from the authenticated route context.
