# Choosing an object

Each illustration is one ordinary office or household object that stands for the idea, drawn plainly. The idea comes from which object and one small detail on it, not from a scene.

What makes them work:

- **One object, one detail.** The papers are tied with string (records kept together); the calendar has one ticked day (the weekly review).
- **At most one companion.** A cup next to the newspaper, a lamp on the desk. More stops reading at card size.
- **Things people recognise from the product.** Tables and stacks for sheets, calendars for routines, a desk for finding your way around.
- **White space is part of the drawing.** Ask for a generous margin.
